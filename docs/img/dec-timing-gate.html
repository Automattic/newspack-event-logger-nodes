A duration nobody measured is not a timing sample

Flame_Builder_Node::accumulate_all_stats() runs two independent gates on every completed request. Read across a row to see what an evicted or aborted request still adds, and what it never touches.

Gate 1 · $count_global (decision 15)

! is_worker

Keeps worker traffic out of every site-wide aggregate, because one long-running job would otherwise dominate the averages. The URL rows keep worker timing in a w: family of their own.

Gate 2 · $record_timing

duration_ms > 0 && error_status ∉ { T, A }

T: Request_Builder_Node::evict_request() stamps it, with due − start, due the stream boundary its window fell due at, on a trace the LRU evicted mid-request. A: Log_Manager stamps it when a cooperative stop lands inside finish(), or when end_job_context() is told the job never finished. Both durations measure time to an interruption, not what the request cost.

So count and timed_count diverge

For a reason that has nothing to do with workers: worker traffic files in a w: family of its own. Stats_Store::mean() divides sum_ms by timed_count, while avg_peak_mb divides by count, and the mean of none is null.

Every average divides the population it summed

stats:totals keeps both counts: global_avg_ms divides sum_ms by the timed count, and global_avg_peak_mb divides sum_peak_mb by requests. A dimension row does the same through DIM_TIMED and DIM_COUNT.
