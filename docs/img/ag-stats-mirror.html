A bucket lives in memory, is summed into memcache every five seconds, and is mirrored once, when it closes

Flame_Builder_Node files each completed request into the five-minute bucket it FINISHED in. Memcache holds the sums; the offsetlog carries the open bucket across a respawn; flame-stats.pN keeps one durable frame per closed bucket, which a dashboard reads back on a memcache miss.

One five-minute bucket, on four tiers

What is mirrored

The aggregates and both per-URL time series in full. Four namespaces are excluded by STATS_MIRROR_TOPN: the three derived coarse tiers, urls_h, urlnames_h and lb_h, each answered from fine buckets the mirror already keeps, and the url flame profiles, rank-capped by set_flame_topn and 0 by default.

The buffer is bounded by MAX_HELD_FRAMES, 10,000 a namespace; past it the lowest-ranked frames are written to the partition EARLY, down to 9,000, rather than dropped. GET_STATS reports mirror_held_frames and mirror_held_bytes.

A respawn

restore_state() takes the carried frames on, and the successor writes them when their bucket closes. The offsetlog is a ring of 60 keyframes, so the open window costs fixed disk there, where flame-stats keeps every frame it receives. The 2 MiB carry is a policy cap set from the observed held size, ~267–348 KB a checkpoint on a production hub, and bounds that ring near 120 MB; a frame past it is re-merged from memcache by the next write to its bucket. The cliff is far above it: a checkpoint record past Partition_Node's MAX_LARGE_LINE_SIZE, 32 MiB, is dropped whole, read cursor and every snapshot node's state included.

Reading it back

Stats_Store hands $rehydrate to its two backed Tables, the aggregate one and the fine urls one: a miss falls through to Partition_Node::locate_by() over the mirror's .idx and lands back in memcache. The per-URL table is not backed: with flame_topn 0 nothing ever wrote a frame to find.

Two versions, two migrations

Memcache migrates by salt rotation (wp nodes memcache flush), which the mirror is built not to feel; its key carries MIRROR_KEY_VERSION instead, bumped on a frame-shape change so a frame filed under the previous version is never read back. A frame is TM_STRUCT with the key ONCE, in Message::KEY; stats-index hashes it from there.
