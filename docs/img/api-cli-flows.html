The two WP-CLI verbs: one reads the firehose back as requests, one prices the hook-storage tiers

Both register under the substrate's nodes namespace from the deferred bootstrap and run @when after_wp_load. reqgrep shares its grouping engine with the performance.grep_requests verb; ruleset-bench is what calibrates Rule_Set::INLINE_HOOK_LIMIT.

1

wp nodes reqgrep [<pattern>]: pick the source

--firehose=<path> is validated before any directory opens: its realpath must sit inside Config::get_logs_directory(), and a path already naming a partition (.p<N>) reads that partition alone. The dirs come from Log_Manager::firehose_dirs().

stdin has dataA pipe or a redirected file, detected by fstat. Wins over every other source: --follow and --recent are ignored.
--followOne Consumer per partition, seeded at the tail, run under the event loop; prints requests as they finish.
otherwise: catOne Consumer per partition from the first segment, or from the second-to-last with --recent.
2

Match each line: Reqgrep_Core

A line matches when its rid equals the pattern exactly, or when compile()'s regex, the pattern preg_quoted and case-insensitive, is found anywhere in the packed envelope. Regex metacharacters carry no meaning. No pattern means ., which every packed line's float timestamp contains.

Not yet matched: the history ringLines of unmatched rids, in --num-buckets (10, clamped 1–100) buckets of --bucket-size (250, clamped 1–10,000) entries, counting rids plus lines; one rid holds at most 10,000 lines in a bucket.
Matched: the in-flight cacheThe rid enters an LRU of 100 items × 3 buckets, rotating every 60 s, and its buffered history is replayed in, so a match on a late line still yields the request from its first line. Per request: 20,000 lines, 10 MiB.

A late match whose earlier lines have rotated out of a FULL ring warns Couldn't find request start in history; a partly filled ring means the reader started mid-request.

3

Print when the request ends

A tracked rid reaching process (complete) or process (aborted) prints as an indented lifecycle tree, or as raw JSONL under --raw; --incomplete keeps only the requests that reached neither. Anything evicted from the oldest in-flight bucket, and anything still open when the run ends, prints with [incomplete].

wp nodes ruleset-bench [--iterations=<n>]

Measurement only, off the request hot path. Every cell of a hooks-per-rule × rule-count grid times three paths --iterations times (default 200, minimum 1) and prints the median in microseconds; a higher count buys a steadier median at the cost of runtime.
