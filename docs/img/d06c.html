The life of a five-minute bucket

Stats_Store writes the URL index one key per server, per shard, per five-minute bucket into the partition's SQLite Tables, and a flush folds each closed hour into a coarse tier: two tiers, two lifetimes. A chart series keeps one key an hour, holding its twelve five-minute slots.

What a key holds

Sums and counts, never means, so buckets and partitions merge by addition and the dashboard divides at read time.

Each producer caps its value before writing it: 50 categories and 20 dimension values a slot, 200 leaderboard categories, and as many URL rows per shard as fit by estimated bytes, each server's URLs under keys of their own. The overflow folds into an Other row, so totals stay exact.

Nothing evicts a row

The Tables are SQLite files on the flame builder's host. A row lives until its time to live passes, and the Router's purge deletes it then.

wp nodes tables flush

Empties the Tables by name and replaces each partition's files. wp nodes memcache flush leaves the statistics alone. They fill again as traffic arrives.
