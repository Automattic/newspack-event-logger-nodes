The Table's key, through Cache_Backend::site_key(). wp nodes memcache flush rotates the salt, the reader hashes the new scope, and weeks of on-disk history stop answering.

The mirror's key carries no scope

evlog:m{V}:p{N}:{key}

Stats_Store::entry_key(), stable across salt rotations. V is MIRROR_KEY_VERSION, the mirror's own migration lever (decision 5).

One function, so nothing disagrees

The mirror write, the stats-index hash, the rehydrate compare and the held-frame lookup all key through entry_key().

restore_state() keeps only keys is_mirror_key() claims, so a checkpoint carried across a key change holds nothing it cannot look up.
