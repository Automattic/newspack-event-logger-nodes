Over 3,840 bytes, the payload shrinks and the category never does

Log_Manager encodes the whole entry, measures that encoding, and only past the cap runs it through fit_data(). Three descending stages: the first two return the first encoding that fits, and the floor is returned unmeasured, so a k over about 2.8 KB still ships oversized. Nothing throws and nothing is chunked: the firehose is fire-and-forget, and the flag is the only symptom.

Each rung cuts m to that many bytes and encodes the whole entry again, because JSON escaping expands bytes and a byte-count subtraction can still land over the cap. The first encoding at or under 3,840 ships; 62 rungs run from 3,840 to 1.

2

Drop m whole

An array m skips the ladder and is dropped outright; a string that never fit is dropped too. Every other key survives.

3

The floor

Reached when the entry still exceeds 3,840 bytes with m gone, because l, caller, k or another key is itself too large: { n, k, ts, truncated: true, m: <first 1,000 bytes of the original encoding> + "..." }. A line no reader can place is worth less than a truncated one.

A span opens and closes on k, and l, the flame's aggregation label, keeps it in the right node through stages 1 and 2; caller is display-only, the detail view's "called by". k is never renamed: a (truncated) suffix would fail Flame_Tree::PATTERN_START and PATTERN_COMPLETE, so a renamed opening never opens and a renamed close orphans the span it should have ended.

The Perl producer bounds a different thing by a different number

Gyrobase::Log holds the whole PACKED LINE to $MAX_LINE_SIZE = 4096, replaces m with the stub (truncated, original N bytes), keeps ts, n, k and whichever of duration_ms / status_code the entry carried, and writes no truncated key at all. It DROPS an oversized job entry with a warning, because half a job structure is not a job.

So a Perl-truncated line reads as whole downstream: Request_Builder_Node copies the flag where it finds one and cannot invent it, and LogEntriesTable prints [truncated] only for a PHP-trimmed entry. A reader must not take the flag's absence as proof a line arrived whole.

Anything that can exceed the cap goes through Job_Intake::queue()

Partition_Node::allow_large_writes() takes the partition's write lock and admits up to 32 MiB. An oversized firehose job arrives at the router with its m stripped, and reads there as an invalid-handler warning.
