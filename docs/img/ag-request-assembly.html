A record is assembled by sequence, evicted by silence, and folded under pressure

Request_Builder_Node keys every firehose line by the envelope's KEY, checks its n against the number it expects next, and holds the record in a three-bucket LRU until the terminal arrives or the LRU evicts it, 400 to 600 seconds after its last line. Two entry bounds fold a record rather than cap it.

The sequence gate, per line

Only process (start) opens a record; an orphan line for an unknown rid drops. Then n is compared with expected_n:

n < expecteda duplicate: dropped, rate-limited INFO
n = expectedstored; expected_n = n + 1
n > expecteda GAP: the first hole is recorded as gap_after; a non-terminal line drops; a terminal still lands, the hole becomes an entries (lost) marker, and process (complete) stamps I

A gyrobase (start) pushes the counter and restarts at 1, so a nested proc_open render under the same rid is no break; its (complete) pops it back. Past MAX_STACK_DEPTH 50 open spans the record is is_runaway: still visible, for Perl gyroscope parity, no longer accumulating, and evicted by the same rotation as any other.

The LRU: 3 buckets × 100, rotating every 200 s or when the newest fills

newestevery get() promotes the rid here
middleuntouched for one rotation
oldestevicted at the next rotation, stamped T

The window measures SILENCE, not duration: a request still emitting lines is re-promoted on each and never ages out. Rotation runs on an absolute 200 s grid, so a silent record goes at the third rotation, 400 to 600 s after its last line, and sooner when a full newest bucket forces one. fire() on the Router's 1 s TIMER rotates the cache too, so a stalled request times out on an idle partition.

Eviction stamps duration_ms as whole seconds since the start, times 1,000, and emits through $this->sink, never a direct file write. A record with no url is dropped on either path.

What leaves, and where

sink the full record, one TM_STRUCT, KEY = rid, on complete or eviction
completed_target the compact summary: method, url, start_time, end_time, duration_ms, status_code, state, error_status, remote_addr, user_agent; the rid rides KEY
errors_target error, warning, stderr, and any *(error) / *(warning)
alerts_target alert entries
inflight_target one row per in-flight request per tick, from the hidden Request_Flight sibling

The fold: a runaway record becomes a path map, and keeps both its ends

fold_request() replays the stored entries through Flame_Fold, keeps FOLD_KEEP_HEAD 10 raw entries (how the request identifies itself: process start, request line, environment) and a rolling FOLD_KEEP_TAIL 10 (how it ends: stats flush, memory, terminal), and drops the list. The request keeps logging; every later entry folds straight into the same path map, so its cost stops tracking message volume. A folded request cannot un-fold.

max_entries_per_request, default 20,000

Constrains ONE envelope and folds that one: the faster trigger for a single runaway. Checked on every append.

entry_budget, default 50,000

Constrains the SUM across everything in flight. relieve_pressure() recounts the truth over the LRU and folds the LARGEST envelope, repeatedly, until under budget; the check re-arms at the budget, or 1,000 entries on when folding could not reach it.

Never a cap

A bound that stopped appending behind a flag nothing surfaces would lose every entry past it. No consumer may reason across the marker: the interval it covers is missing detail, not idle time, and entries (lost), the marker a sequence gap leaves, is read the same way.
