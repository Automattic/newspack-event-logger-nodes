The complete worker, one partition of it

Four Consumers, two of them tailing what another branch wrote. Solid boxes are Nodes the topology declares; dashed boxes are partition logs on disk; green is memcache. One firehose Consumer feeds two branches through a Tee, and a job crosses two logs, an ingress log and jobs.pN, before a handler runs it.
